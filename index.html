LPU PROJECT • SMART WASTE RECOVERY

Turn household waste into recoverable resources.

ReWaste is a simple digital solution for poor household waste segregation. It helps citizens know what goes where, improves collection, and connects separated waste with recycling and recovery.

Explore the solution Try the demo

Know your waste

Segregate at source so valuable material doesn't get lost in mixed waste.

🟢 Wet Waste

Food scraps, vegetable peels, organic waste

🔵 Dry Waste

Paper, cardboard, plastics, metals

🟤 Garden Waste

Leaves, grass and other biodegradable material

🔴 Special Waste

E-waste, batteries and hazardous household items

The problem

When different types of household waste are thrown together, recyclable materials become contaminated and difficult or impossible to recover.

🗑️

Mixed at source

Residents often put wet, dry and special waste into the same bag because segregation is inconvenient or confusing.

🔄

Recovery is lost

Paper, plastic, metal and other valuable materials can become contaminated by food and moisture.

🌍

Environmental cost

More mixed waste reaches dumpsites, increasing pollution while useful resources are wasted.

A practical digital solution

ReWaste combines education, simple classification and collection support into one platform.

1

2

3

Keep separated waste ready for scheduled collection or a recovery centre.

4

Recover

Recyclable materials can enter recycling/recovery streams instead of being lost.
